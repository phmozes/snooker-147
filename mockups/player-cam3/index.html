The two follow options (V1 and V2 only)

  • F1 "turn": 0.1 s after the cue hits the cue ball, the camera turns in 0.8 s (a smooth start and stop) to look at the pocket the shot aims at. It may slide up to 80 cm to a spot on the line pocket → ball, behind the ball and at most 1.6 m from the pocket (25 cm to the side if that spot is in front of the ball, so the ball never rolls under the lens). It keeps its height (V1 16 cm, V2 55 cm, or more where a fix lifted it) and zooms in so the view is about 80 cm wide at the pocket.
  • F2 "turn and lift": the same turn and slide, but it also rises to 30° above the cloth (seen from the pocket) and comes to at most 1.5 m from the pocket, so you look down into the pocket and see the drop.
  • Safe path (the H2/H3 rules): the move is an arc around the moving look-at point (never top-down, the steepest angle in any clip is the 57° of the main camera); it never slides further outside the cloth than the hold spot; outside the cloth it stays at least 10 cm above the rail; it never goes lower than its hold height.
  • Then R2: when the balls stop, a 0.4 s glide back to 57°, and SHOOT waits for it.

Fair for a pot and a miss

  • The follow uses only the planned shot: which ball (target), which pocket it is played at (pocket), where that ball stands before the shot, and the pocket's mouth. It never reads the recorded roll of the balls, where they stop, or whether anything dropped.
  • The timing is fixed from the hit (start 0.1 s, move 0.8 s), the same for every shot. A pot and a miss give the same camera move; only the balls themselves show the result, when they reach the pocket.
  • The only thing that differs is when the camera goes back to 57°: when the balls stop. That is the same as today.
