147 – miss sound options

The crowd's "ooh" / groan of disappointment after a miss (1.5 s each). Tap a button to hear it, pick A, B or C. All three are set to the same loudness. Not in the game yet: later it plays only once the miss is clear on screen.

Miss

A: a group groan. B: a crowd "oh…", let down. C: a crowd sigh of disappointment (softer, more distant).
