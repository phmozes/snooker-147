Night 7 (2026-11-27): why 6 reds plays worse with the v3 break-off. Diagnosis only, nothing in the game changed.
Script: bench/night7/reds6-diag.test.ts (PART=lib -> reds6-lib.txt, PART=rerack -> reds6-rerack.txt).

1. The 5 RE-RACK rounds (v3 soak) all share one thing: the last red (step 11) is the apex red, 2 mm in front of the
   pink, on the centre line, where the break left it. It never moved during the round (0.0 cm). With the pink behind
   it no pocket can take it (allRedsLocked = YES), so the solver finds no shot. Only 3 different breaks: entry 163
   (yellow side, speed 5.34) 3 times, entry 128 (yellow, 5.57) and entry 27 (green, 5.66) once each.

2. Old vs v3 6-red library (200 red-pottable breaks each):
   pack after the break: fully open in both (packed reds mean 0.04 vs 0.00 of 6)
   pottable reds at step 1: mean 2.44 vs 2.39; natural success shots at steps 2-3: 49% vs 51% (soak)
   a red touching the pink: 68% vs 92% of breaks; a red no pocket can take: 93% vs 97%
   cue ball ends in the top half: 67% vs 77% (median y 0.24 vs 0.36 m)
   Long shots: all 48 (old) and all 65 (v3) come from rounds with a locked red; 0 in rounds without one. Most are
   "overLong" colours at steps 8-10 (v3: blue at step 10, 19 times; old: 2).
3. Start side and break speed: no clear effect. Red on pink by side: v3 green 95% / yellow 90%; by speed slow 88%,
   mid 93%, fast 96% (old: 58-75%). Long shots per side flip between the libraries (old: yellow worse, v3: green worse),
   so that is chance. All 5 RE-RACKs had a fast break (5.34-5.66), but they are only 3 different breaks.
Cause: from the baulk line the break hits the outer red and leaves the apex red resting against the pink more often
(92% vs 68%). That red is usually freed later by a cannon, but sometimes it is the last red, and then RE-RACK. 3 of
the 5 came from one break entry, so part of the 5-vs-1 gap is chance.
